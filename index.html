      About this map

      Faultline indexes publicly disclosed reports from HackerOne bug bounty and vulnerability disclosure programs. For every report it keeps the title, the program, the weakness the reporter or triager tagged, the bounty paid and the number of upvotes. Every title links to the full write-up on HackerOne.

      The report list comes from the open hackerone-reports dataset on GitHub. HackerOne tags reports with about 215 different weakness names, many close to CWE entries. Faultline sorts them into 18 broader classes so related bugs sit together. Reports with no weakness tag are grouped as Untagged.

      The CVE page lists every CVE ID that appears in a report title, linked to its entry in the US National Vulnerability Database. Reports that don't name their CVE in the title aren't on that page.

      Bounty totals count only amounts disclosed in public reports. Many programs pay bounties privately, so the real totals are higher.

      The Platforms page lists public programs on HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti and YesWeHack, with payout ranges and in-scope targets, from the open bounty-targets-data project, which refreshes from the platforms every 30 minutes. Private, invite-only programs aren't included. Other platforms don't publish disclosed reports openly, so the report map covers HackerOne only.
